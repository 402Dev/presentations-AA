Case 001

Pejman

He is not learning German for work yet. He wants it so life in Switzerland feels less foreign and more shared.

Switzerland German social integration absolute beginner Persian / Farsi
Starting Conditions
01 Setting Pejman lives in Switzerland.
02 Immediate Aim He wants German for better integration into the society around him, not for work yet.
03 Starting Level He is an absolute beginner.
04 Linguistic Base His first language is Persian, or Farsi.

Public transport first

Where to begin

Start with informal, everyday talk.

It fits his goal and his level. He wants social integration. He is still an absolute beginner.

informal everyday short exchanges low pressure public transport
Thought process
01 Start small Do not start with formal German. Start with short informal interactions.
02 Find the places Where do such interactions happen most? Restaurant, supermarket, or public transport.
03 Choose one Public transport is a strong first example. It is frequent, structured, and easy to repeat.
04 Frequent interaction A ticket check is common. In Swiss German usage, the everyday word for ticket is often Billet. A SwissPass may also be shown.

Query for slot 1

The handoff

We search for a short phrase for the first empty slot: handing over the ticket.

What we ask for

Search for a handoff phrase

Situation A person gives something to another person. Here, it is the ticket.
Speaker move The learner is following the checker's request and showing the object.
Phrase type We want a fixed phrase: a ready-made block you do not build from scratch.

Query for slot 2

The polite close

We now search for a short phrase for the second empty slot: closing the exchange politely.

What we ask for

Search for a closing phrase

Situation The checker ends politely, and the learner answers in the same spirit.
Speaker move The learner returns the polite gesture and closes the exchange without sounding rude.
Phrase type We want a very small phrase, not a full sentence like "I also wish you a nice day."
